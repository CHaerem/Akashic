Privacy

Last updated 24 July 2026

Akashic is a personal journey album. Its architecture is deliberately light, and so is this policy: we operate no servers of our own and we hold no accounts for you.

Where your data lives

Your journeys, days, and photos are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Akashic reads and writes that data on your device with your Apple ID; it does not pass through any server we run. We cannot see your private journeys or your original photos.

What we collect

  • No analytics of our own. We run no trackers, no advertising SDKs, and no behavioural profiling in the app or on this website. The one thing we do not control is Mapbox, which powers the map — see “Maps and photos” below.
  • No accounts held by us. Sign-in uses your Apple ID directly with Apple; we never receive your password and keep no user database.
  • No third-party data sales. We have no data to sell.

Public journeys

When an owner explicitly publishes a journey, a copy of that journey's details and photo thumbnails is written to a world-readable area of iCloud so it can be viewed as a public page. This only happens by the owner's deliberate action, and only the published content is visible — never private journeys and never full-resolution originals. An owner can unpublish at any time from the app.

Maps and photos

The globe and maps are rendered using Mapbox GL. Loading a map sends standard web requests (such as your IP address) to Mapbox to fetch map tiles. Mapbox GL also sends its own anonymous usage and diagnostic events to Mapbox and stores a randomly generated identifier in your browser to do so — this is Mapbox's own telemetry, subject to Mapbox's privacy terms. It is not something we run, receive, or use, and it is limited to pages that load the map. Published photo thumbnails are served from the owner's iCloud.

Your rights

Because your data lives in your iCloud, you remain in control of it: export or delete it from within the app at any time. To report or request takedown of a published public journey, email report@akashic.no.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@akashic.no.
