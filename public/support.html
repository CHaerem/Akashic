Akashic is a personal journey album — plot your treks and travels on a 3D globe, add photos and notes day by day, and share a published journey as a public page. Your data stays in your own iCloud.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

In your own iCloud account. Akashic uses Apple's CloudKit, so your journeys and photos live under your Apple ID — not on any server we run. We have no copy of your private content.

How does sharing work?

Each journey is private until you publish it. Publishing makes that journey's details and photo thumbnails world-readable, so anyone with the link can view it as a public page. Full-resolution originals are never published — only thumbnails.

How do I unpublish a journey?

Open the journey in the app and turn off publishing. The public page stops being served. You stay in control of what is public.

How do I export my data?

Use the export option in the app to get your journeys and photos out. Because the data is in your iCloud, it is yours to keep and move.

How do I report a public journey?

Every public page has a Report content link, or email report@akashic.no with the journey and a reason. See our Terms for the takedown policy.

Need help? Email support@akashic.no and we'll get back to you.
